## What a reviewer checks

1. The 15 family names and the 66 sub-family identifiers and titles in `data/iar-v1.1-subfamilies.csv` against the
   official IAR v1.1.
2. The priorities against Annex B.
3. A sample of sub-family mappings, with the crosswalk's rationale, against the IAR and ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
4. That [limitations.md](limitations.md) tells users what they need to know.

## How to record a review

Open an issue titled `Review: UAE IAR v1.1` with your name or handle, your relevant experience, the scope you
checked and the official copy you used. Each disagreement is one line: item, current value, proposed value, reason.
Mapping changes are made in the crosswalk first, then the snapshot here is refreshed and the change recorded in
[framework-notes.md](framework-notes.md).
