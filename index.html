Quick start & methodology · Sources, mapping rules and sample data

For each control family, record the implementation status, the owner, a target date and the evidence you could show an assessor. The summary, the gap list and the per-family bars update as you type, and a draft is kept in this browser until you reset it. Export JSON to keep a dated record, or CSV to work in a spreadsheet.

The matrix opens at family level. Work at the 66 IAR v1.1 sub-families replaces the families with the regulation's sub-families (identifiers, short titles, priorities and mappings), or import your own control list with Import controls (CSV) (template). Mappings are derived from the verified UAE IAR overlay of the GRC Control Crosswalk. Read the sources and mapping rules, the limitations and the dated worked example.
